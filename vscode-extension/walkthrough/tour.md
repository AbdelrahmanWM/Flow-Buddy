# The tour

`flowbuddy tour` opens in a terminal and takes a few minutes:

1. What Flow Buddy does, and what it keeps (counts and habits; never what you type)
2. Whether everything's running
3. A sample tip on your screen, and how to answer it
4. Where tips show
5. The five settings that matter most
6. The daily lesson, practice and the skill map
7. Pausing, quieter modes and quiet hours

Press Enter to go on, or q to stop.
