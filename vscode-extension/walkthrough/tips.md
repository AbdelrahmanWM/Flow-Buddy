# Where tips show

The **Flow Buddy: Tips** setting:

- **desktop** (the default): Flow Buddy's card, under the top bar, with the whole tip, what its commands do, and the answers.
- **notification**: a VS Code notification at the bottom right with **Got it**, **Not helpful** and **What does it do?**, and the tip highlighted in the status bar.
- **statusBar**: only the status bar. Hover for the whole tip; click to answer.

**Not helpful** makes that tip come less often.
