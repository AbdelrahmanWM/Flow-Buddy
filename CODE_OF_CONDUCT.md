# Code of conduct

Flow Buddy follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, and assume good faith. Criticise ideas, not people. People learning Vim are exactly who this project is for, so no question is too basic. No harassment, insults or personal attacks, in any project space: issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, contact the maintainer privately through the repository's **Security** tab → **Report a vulnerability**, and say it's a conduct report. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may remove comments, lock threads or ban accounts, as the Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
